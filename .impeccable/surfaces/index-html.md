## Scope

The main site (`index.html`, all tabs: sign-in, Teams/spin wheel, Quiz, Games/bracket, Organiser). Mode: **Operate**. Phone-first (confirmed by the user 2026-10-24); the TV quiz screen (`tv.html`) follows the same world.

## Audience and job

About 20 adults, mostly middle-aged parents, on phones in short bursts: sign in, spin for a team, add a quiz question, vote, follow the bracket. The organiser runs the draw and the tournament.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is what tumbles out of a pulled Christmas cracker: tissue crown, joke slip, foil tube. It refuses the default app look of soft cards on a pale ground with a green header.

OWN-WORLD: Deep cracker red (#9E1028-ish) owns the page as the ground. A gold foil band with crimped zigzag ends frames the masthead. Content sits on white "joke slips": square-cornered paper with a thin red print rule and slip numbers. Each team is a tissue-crown colour (red, gold, emerald, cobalt, plum, tangerine) and its badge has a zigzag crown edge. Display type is a chunky rounded face; body is a sturdy readable sans. The only shadows are short and hard, like paper.

STORY: Open the site, see "A Xmas for the Ages" on a gold cracker band. Your job is always the biggest thing on the screen (spin, add a question, vote). Results feel like prizes: your team arrives as a crown.

FIRST VIEWPORT (phone 390): a cracker-band masthead with crimped ends, the site name in two chunky lines, and a "62 sleeps" tag; tabs as foil tags; then the Teams wheel at full width with the "Spin for my team" button under it, and the team board below.

FORM: candidate 5 of 7, paper crowns and joke slips, within the user-pinned "Christmas cracker" world; seed 87b0a1c1. Raise from the timetable rack: rank by weight, case and reversal on a strict small type scale, not by many sizes. Raise from the design annual: one consistent rule language (a 2px red print rule on every slip).
SIGNATURE: when the spin lands, gold and tissue-coloured confetti bursts from the wheel and a crown badge drops onto your team's slip. It stays still under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
